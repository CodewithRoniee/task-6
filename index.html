Before you buy

Three things that matter more than the plant itself

Light comes before water

Most houseplant deaths trace back to the wrong spot in the room, not a missed watering. We note the light each plant actually needs, not the light it can technically survive.

Underwater, don't overwater

Nearly every plant we sell would rather be forgotten for a week than watered on a fixed schedule. Check the soil, not the calendar.

Repot in spring, not panic

A plant that looks unhappy usually needs a different windowsill before it needs a bigger pot. Wait for active growth before you disturb the roots.
